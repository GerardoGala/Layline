Laylines

The objective of this simulation is to develop the student's ability to understand and use laylines when sailing toward a windward mark.

A layline is the course sailed on a tack that will take the boat directly to the Windward Mark. The student will learn to recognize when to continue sailing and when to tack to make an efficient approach to the mark.

Instructions

  1. Sail from the starting position toward the Windward Mark.
  2. The simulation uses a steady 15-knot wind.
  3. Use the ILCA WIND VANE to determine the wind direction and observe your boat's heading.
  4. Decide when to Tack so that your new course will take you toward the Windward Mark.
  5. Avoid tacking too early or sailing beyond the layline.
  6. Your goal is to make an efficient approach to the Windward Mark using the correct layline.

Three Stages of the Simulation

The simulation is designed to take you from understanding the concept of a layline to making the decision yourself. Each stage removes a little more assistance.

Stage 1 — Learn

The layline is visible. Use it to understand the relationship between the wind direction, your boat's course, and the Windward Mark.

Stage 2 — Practice

The layline is hidden. Use what you learned in Stage 1 to decide when to tack. The simulation provides feedback so you can improve your judgment.

Stage 3 — Challenge

The assistance is removed. Use the wind, your boat's heading, and the position of the Windward Mark to make your own tack decision.
